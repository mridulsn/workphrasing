About workphrasing

workphrasing turns a heated message into a calm, professional email, at the spice level you choose.

It runs entirely in your browser. What you type is never sent anywhere, and your message is never stored. Only your spice level and the name for your sign-off are remembered, on this device.

Your own words are never copied into the email, so nothing rude slips through.

Smart mode appears only when your Chrome has its own on-device AI. It also stays on your device.
