About workphrasing

workphrasing reads how a heated work message feels, then writes a calm one you can send: short enough for chat, with one clear ask. Switch to Email when you need a subject line and sign-off.

The feeling it shows is a guess from your words, and it's only for you. It never goes into the message. If it guesses wrong, pick the right one.

It runs entirely in your browser. What you type is never sent anywhere or stored. Only your settings and your sign-off name are remembered, on this device. Your own words are never copied into the message, so nothing rude slips through.

Smart mode appears only when your Chrome has its own on-device AI. It also stays on your device.
