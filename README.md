# workphrasing

Type what you really want to say at work. workphrasing reads how you're feeling and writes a calm,
short message you can paste into Slack, Teams, WhatsApp or a text. There's an email version too.

Live: https://mridulsaravanan.com/workphrasing/

- **Feeling first.** It guesses the feeling behind the message (frustrated, angry, overwhelmed, worried, hurt,
  disappointed, impatient or confused) and shows the words that gave it away. You can correct the guess with one tap.
- **Chat rules.** Each message is 1-3 sentences with one clear ask. There's no bare "Hi" and no sign-off.
- **Settings.** Choose who it's for (teammate, manager, client or group chat) and how direct it should be
  (Warm, Straight or Firm). A passive-aggressive mode is there just for laughs.
- **Private.** It runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved, and your own words are
  never copied into the message.

`engine.js` holds the phrasebook and the feeling detector. `index.html` is the page. The fonts are Figtree and
Pixelify Sans, both self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License (`assets/fonts/OFL-Figtree.txt`,
`assets/fonts/OFL-PixelifySans.txt`).
